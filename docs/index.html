AndroidMeasure Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Short version: AndroidMeasure collects zero data. It has no internet permission, no analytics, no telemetry, no ads, and no accounts.

1. Data Collection

AndroidMeasure does not collect, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal data, usage data, crash telemetry, or device identifiers. The app does not even request the Android INTERNET permission.

2. Camera & Sensors

The app requests access to your device camera solely to run on-device ARCore depth estimation and 3D measurement calculations in real time. Camera frames and sensor readings stay strictly in memory on your phone and are never transmitted anywhere.

3. Photos & Exports

When you tap the Photo button, the annotated measurement image is saved locally to your device (Pictures/AndroidMeasure and the app's offline history). Exports (photos, PDFs, or CSV files) leave your device only when you explicitly choose to share them through Android's system share sheet.

4. User Feedback

If you choose to send feedback from Settings, AndroidMeasure opens your own email app with only the text description you typed. No screenshots, logs, or telemetry are attached automatically. Any email you choose to send is handled by your email provider like normal email.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact the developer using the support email listed on the AndroidMeasure Google Play Store page.
